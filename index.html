Situated on the highly sought-after Laver Drive in Tapton, this exceptional first-floor corner apartment offers stylish contemporary living in a prime residential location. Constructed in 2023 and presented to an immaculate standard throughout, this impressive home extends to approximately 690 sq. ft., making it an ideal purchase for first-time buyers, professionals, couples or investors alike.

The accommodation opens with a spacious entrance hallway featuring a generous built-in storage cupboard. At the heart of the home is a superb dual-aspect open-plan living, dining and kitchen space with integrated dishwasher and washing machine, flooded with natural light and enjoying an attractive Juliet balcony. The contemporary kitchen is finished to a high specification with sleek cabinetry, integrated appliances and ample worktop space, creating the perfect environment for both everyday living and entertaining.

The principal bedroom provides a spacious and peaceful retreat and benefits from a stylish en-suite shower room. A luxurious family bathroom, finished to the same high standard, completes the accommodation.

Further benefits include double glazing, gas central heating via a combination boiler, an allocated parking space and additional visitor parking.

Perfectly positioned, the property enjoys easy access to Chesterfield Train Station, Chesterfield College, the town centre and its excellent range of shops, restaurants and leisure facilities. Tapton Park and picturesque walks are also just moments away, making this an outstanding opportunity to acquire a modern home in one of Chesterfield's most desirable residential locations.
